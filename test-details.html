CBC (Complete Blood Count)

Purpose: Detects infections, anemia, and blood disorders.

Sample Required: Blood Sample.

Preparation: No special preparation required.

Normal Reference Range: WBC: 4,000–11,000 cells/µL.

Report Delivery Time: Within 24 Hours.

Important Instructions: Stay hydrated before the test.
Fasting Requirement: Not Required.
Consult your doctor for result interpretation.

HbA1c (Glycated Hemoglobin)

Purpose: Measures average blood sugar over the last 3 months.

Sample Required: Blood Sample.

Preparation: No fasting required.

Normal Reference Range: Below 5.7%.

Report Delivery Time: Within 24 Hours.

Important Instructions: Inform the doctor if you have diabetes.
Fasting Requirement: Not Required.
Used for diabetes diagnosis and monitoring.

LFT (Liver Function Test)

Purpose: Evaluates liver health and function.

Sample Required: Blood Sample.

Preparation: Avoid alcohol before the test.

Normal Reference Range: ALT: 7–56 U/L.

Report Delivery Time: Within 24 Hours.

Important Instructions: Inform the laboratory about medications.
Fasting Requirement: 8–10 Hours Preferred.
Helps diagnose liver diseases.

KFT (Kidney Function Test)

Purpose: Evaluates kidney performance.

Sample Required: Blood Sample.

Preparation: Drink adequate water before testing.

Normal Reference Range: Creatinine: 0.6–1.3 mg/dL.

Report Delivery Time: Within 24 Hours.

Important Instructions: Avoid heavy exercise before the test.
Fasting Requirement: Not Required.
Used to assess kidney health.

ECG (Electrocardiogram)

Purpose: Records the electrical activity of the heart.

Sample Required: No blood sample required.

Preparation: Wear comfortable clothing.

Normal Reference Range: Heart Rate: 60–100 bpm.

Report Delivery Time: Immediate.

Important Instructions: Remain relaxed during the procedure.
Fasting Requirement: Not Required.
Used to detect heart rhythm abnormalities.
